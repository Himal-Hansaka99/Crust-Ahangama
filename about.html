ABOUT CRUST

MORE THAN
A RESTAURANT.

Crust started as a wood-fired oven and a folding table. It grew into a bar, a pool hall, a stage and a shop — because Ahangama asked for all of it.

OUR FOOD

Fire first.
Always.

Every plate that leaves the kitchen has touched the wood-fired oven at some point — pizza, sides, even dessert. It's the one rule the kitchen won't break.

OUR BAR

Arrack, done
properly.

We built the cocktail list around Ceylon arrack first, imported spirits second. It's the drink of this coastline, and it deserves better than a mixer.

OUR COMMUNITY

Strangers at 7.
Regulars by 9.

Surfers, digital nomads, local families, first-time tourists — the pool tables and the bar have a way of putting everyone at the same table by the end of the night.

THE AHANGAMA VIBE

A town built
around the water

Ahangama runs on surf reports and sunset plans. Crust exists at the point where a day at the break turns into a night that doesn't have a clear ending.
